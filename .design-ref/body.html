Why owners keep coming back

We are a small shoppe, and that is the whole point

The Barker Shoppe opened in 2015 to give Springfield owners somewhere their dog is genuinely looked after, not just housed until pickup. We stayed small on purpose. The same people greet your dog every morning.

Dogs only

No cats, no small animals. Every yard, kennel, and routine here is built around dogs.
