Dog Daycare

Dogs come in, get sorted into a playgroup that suits them, and spend the day between the turf yard and a rest break. Most owners book it for the days they are gone longest, and half days cover the shorter ones.

Playgroups by size

Sorted by size and social level, so the shy ones are not stuck with the bulldozers.

Half or full days

Pick what fits your shift. Regulars use multi-day passes instead of paying per visit.

Play and rest on rotation

Dogs go home tired instead of wound up, and nobody runs all day without a break.

Food and meds welcome

Send anything your dog takes during the day and we handle it at the right time.

More about daycare Full day $30, half day $15, packages from $19 a day.
Open weekends and holidays

Overnight Boarding

Bring their own food and medication and we keep the routine they already know. Dogs from the same house bunk together when that is what they are used to, and everyone gets yard time during the day, not just kennel time.

Every 7th night free

Long trips cost less. The discount applies automatically on the seventh night.

Siblings together

Dogs from the same home can share a run instead of being split up.

Their food, their schedule

Send meals portioned however you like and note any medication at drop-off.

Weekend pickup hours

Saturday and Sunday, 8–11 am and 4–6 pm for drop-off and pickup.

Before your first visit

What every dog needs, whatever they are booked for

The same three vaccinations apply to daycare, boarding, and grooming. Your vet can email records straight to us before you come in.

Bordetella Required and current for every dog in the building
DHPP Distemper combination, current on the date of the visit
Rabies Proof of a current rabies vaccination
Bring along Food for boarding stays, plus any medication with dosing notes

Paperwork is filled out here at drop-off and takes a few minutes. There is no account to set up online.
